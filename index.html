03About

Behind thetimeline.

Bengaluru, INEnglish · ಕನ್ನಡ

I'm Hanumant — a video editor who works as comfortably in a prompt window as on a timeline.

I trained in Graphics & VFX at Arena Animation / Jain University, and since then I've cut everything from gaming highlight montages and pet-brand teasers to podcasts, technology explainers and medical awareness Reels.

Right now I edit technology-focused videos for YouTube and Instagram, and run Kalpha Studios, where I produce healthcare marketing content for doctors and clinics across North Karnataka — clinic promos, Reels, patient testimonials, and Kannada-language awareness videos made for regional audiences.

My process starts before the edit. I use AI to research topics, structure scripts and draft hooks, then shape the story in Premiere Pro, After Effects or DaVinci Resolve. When a project calls for it, I generate footage with Higgsfield AI, Runway and Pika, or build videos programmatically with Remotion — no camera or crew required.

The goal is the same every time: take an idea or a script and turn it into something clear, well-paced and worth watching to the end.
